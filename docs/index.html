I

What Substancia is

Not a wiki mirror — a purpose-built visual interface, and a full pharmacology reference underneath it.

The Firmament

Every specimen placed by effect similarity, not alphabet or drug class — explore by feeling.

Specimen plates

Dose ranges, duration arcs, tolerance, addiction potential, neurochemistry, and cited prose.

Hazard callouts

Fatal-interaction and carcinogenicity warnings pulled into their own boxed callouts — never buried.

Kinship & divergence

Nearest specimens by shared effects, and separately by shared receptor targets — and where they disagree.

Diptych compare

Any two specimens, side by side.

Fully local

No account, no telemetry, no network calls beyond loading the bundled dataset.

III

Before you download

Unsigned build

Substancia isn't code-signed — a certificate costs several hundred dollars a year, and every cent this project has goes to keeping the app itself free instead. That means Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper will flag it as from an unrecognized publisher on first launch.

It's safe. On Windows, click More info → Run anyway. On macOS, right-click the app and choose Open. The source is public — verify it yourself any time.

V

Keep it free

Substancia will always be free — every specimen, every feature, for everyone. That only stays true if the work behind it is sustainable: compiling and cleaning a 288-specimen dataset, building the visual layer on top of it, and maintaining it across three platforms is real, ongoing work. Every dollar from Ko-fi goes back into keeping the app itself free instead of putting a price on it.
